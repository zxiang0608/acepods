# Ace Pods — Growth Research Findings & Decisions

Started 2026-07-06. Source: Brainbox market-research prompt chain, Step 1 (Deep Research), run in parallel on Gemini and ChatGPT. This file tracks what's agreed, what's decided, and what's still open — update it as we go, don't restate the full reports.

## 1. Business context used for the research (agreed inputs)

- **Product:** Acoustic office pods — manufacture + install. Models: Solo (RM12,500), Plus (RM14,500), Flex (RM19,900), Flex Duo (RM23,900), Meet (price varies by spec), Hub (RM27,800 upper bound). 100% Malaysian-made. Factory + showroom in Klang, Selangor.
- **Customer:** HR/ops managers at MNCs and mid-size companies; business owners directly at SMEs (decision-maker chain is an Assumption, not verified).
- **Business stage:** 180+ pods installed since 2023, but leads dropped ~83% (≈12/month → ≈2/month) over the last 2–3 months (approx. April/May 2026 onward).
- **Channels today:** Organic Google only. No paid ads, no FB/IG/TikTok/XHS. WhatsApp is primary conversion path.
- **Budget for new channel testing:** RM900/month, strict.
- **Known competitors (confirmed real):** supreme.com.my, instapod.co/my, tekkashop.com.my, officepro.my, enbooth.com, soundboxacoustic.com.
- **Site context:** Live production site had continuous major changes (schema, pricing, product data, new SEO pages) from ~May 10 to June 19, 2026 — directly overlapping the start of the lead decline. Not yet confirmed as cause vs. coincidence.

## 2. Deep Research — where Gemini and ChatGPT agreed (high confidence)

- **Final verdict, both reports: "Proceed with caution."**
- **Root cause is more likely internal/technical than a market collapse.** No evidence of a demand crash; both point at the May–June site changes as prime suspect. **Both say: check Search Console / GA4 / GBP Insights before spending anything.**
- **Ace is not overpriced.** Enbooth is cheaper (~RM9,999) but weaker acoustically (20dB vs Ace's certified -27dB); Supreme/Tekkashop overlap or exceed Ace's range. "Too expensive" is more likely a trust/messaging issue than a sticker-price issue.
- **RM900/month rules out broad Google/Meta ads** — both independently did the CPC math and concluded it burns the budget with no usable signal.
- **First channel to test: specifier/partner outreach** (interior designers, renovation contractors, coworking operators) — both landed here independently.
- **Government/GLC segment is real but slow** — MOF vendor registration / ePerolehan procurement is the gatekeeper, not a quick win.
- **Halal is not material** to this category.
- **Confirmed (both reports, independently): Supreme Office Furniture's site has copy reading "At ACE Pods Malaysia & Ace Workplace Solution, we supply high-quality office pods..."** — real plagiarism of Ace's own brand copy, found on `supreme.com.my/collections/-office-phone-pod-malaysia`.

## 4b. Real-world correction to the segment table (from founder experience, 2026-07-06)

Gemini's report ranked **"Boutique Interior Design & Build Contractors" as the #1 opportunity segment**, assuming high willingness to pay since cost is absorbed into the client's renovation budget. Founder's actual experience contradicts this: the recurring pattern with ID/architect inquiries is **quote → ask for a better price → either proceed or go silent, or quote → go silent immediately.** Example: an architect (Arkitek LLA) requested a quote for 28 single pods for a project in Penang — sent to a competitor (Supreme), seen by us, illustrates the pattern rather than being our own lead. Founder's read: "IDs usually go for the cheapest." (User-provided fact / direct operating experience — outweighs Gemini's Assumption-labeled willingness-to-pay claim for this segment.)

**Implication:** Downgrade confidence in the ID/specifier channel as a high-value segment until proven otherwise. Treat it as price-competitive and low-commitment, not a "high willingness to pay" opportunity as Gemini's report assumed.

## 4c. Identity/story decision — resolved (2026-07-06)

Three candidate brand stories (StoryBrand SB7) were drafted: (1) Verifiable Decision — fear of choosing the wrong/untrustworthy vendor; (2) Flexible Asset — lease/CapEx angle for facilities/procurement; (3) Team You're Responsible For — HR guilt over unaddressed complaints.

**Key fact that resolved it:** Founder confirms buyers who contact Ace have almost always already decided they want a pod, and already researched via AI search (ChatGPT etc.) before reaching out. (User-provided fact / founder experience.)

**Conclusion:** By the time a buyer makes contact, the "do we need a pod" question (Story 3's territory) is already answered — likely by AI-search content upstream. What's actually being decided at first contact is "which vendor do I trust," which is Story 1's territory, and matches the one directly-validated founder-interview finding (deals won/lost on trust/verification, not price or awareness).

**Decision: Story 1 (Verifiable Decision) is the primary brand story for all sales-facing touchpoints** (website, WhatsApp, showroom conversation). Story 3's emotional register is not discarded — it may still fit future top-of-funnel/awareness content aimed at people who haven't decided yet, but that is a separate, later workstream, not the core identity. Story 2 (Flexible Asset) remains a secondary supporting layer for facilities/procurement stakeholders later in the approval chain.

## 4d. Target customer decision — resolved (2026-07-06)

Ran the Step 2 (Target Audience) scoring exercise on 5 candidate segments (Profitability 25% / Ease of Reach 25% / Pain-Urgency 25% / Competition 10% / Fit 15%):

| Segment | Weighted score | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| **HR/Ops at Mid-Large MNCs (current core)** | **7.0 — chosen** | Strong |
| Coworking/serviced-office operators | 6.25 | Weak-Moderate, disputed between reports |
| SME business owners (direct) | 6.2 | Moderate |
| Boutique ID/build contractors | 4.4 | Downgraded — real experience contradicts research assumption |
| GLC / Government | 3.45 | Strong facts, impractical timeline |

**Decision: HR/Ops managers at mid-to-large MNCs remains the priority target segment.** Not the highest-scoring on reach alone, but the only segment with an existing structural head start (180+ installs, MNC client logos, AI-search/GEO position, and the Story 1 narrative already built around this exact buyer). Coworking operators and SME owners remain open for cheap tests, not a resourcing shift. ID/contractors and GLC/Government explicitly deprioritized (see 4b for ID correction).

**So, as of this session: positioning (category + narrative) and target customer are both decided.**
