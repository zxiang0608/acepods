# Google Review Outreach — WhatsApp Message Templates

**Goal:** Get to ~15–20 Google reviews. Currently trailing InstaPod (126 reviews) — this is the #1 AI-citation gap.  
**Cadence:** 2–3 messages per week, starting with your warmest relationships first  
**Link to use:** Your Google Business Profile review link (get it from GBP dashboard → Get more reviews → Share review form)

---

## Template A — Warm / known well (best for Parker, CMA CGM, Taylor's, etc.)

> Hi [Name], hope you're doing well!
>
> Quick favour — we're building up our Google reviews and I'd love to get one from you, since you've seen our work firsthand at [company name].
>
> It only takes 2 minutes. Here's the direct link: [REVIEW LINK]
>
> Anything honest is great — just what your experience was like. Really appreciate it 🙏

---

> Direct link if you have 2 minutes: [REVIEW LINK]

>
> One small thing — if you have 2 minutes, a Google review would mean a lot to us. We're a local Malaysian manufacturer and reviews are how new buyers find and trust us.
>
> Link here: [REVIEW LINK]
>

---

## What to do before sending

1. Get your Google review link from GBP (Google Business Profile dashboard → "Get more reviews")
2. List your installed clients by warmth: warm (know them well) → medium → cold
3. Start with your top 5–8 warmest clients in Week 1
4. Space messages 2–3 per week — don't blast everyone at once (looks unnatural to Google)

## What to do if they don't respond after 1 week

Send one follow-up only:

> Hi [Name], just a gentle nudge on the Google review — [REVIEW LINK] — no worries if it's not convenient. Thanks either way!

---

## What makes a review more useful for AI search

Ask them to mention **specific details** if they're willing:
- The pod model (Ace Solo, Ace Meet, etc.)
- The acoustic quality / soundproofing
- The install experience
- Company name and location (e.g. "for our Kuala Lumpur office")

Reviews with specifics get cited by AI engines. Generic "great service!" reviews help Google stars but don't help GEO/AI search visibility.
