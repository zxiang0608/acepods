# Design System and Layout Rules — Ace Workplace Solutions

## 1. Overall visual direction
The site should feel:
- premium architectural interior brand
- warm near-white
- calm
- refined
- minimal
- expensive but attainable
- commercially clear

Do not make it feel like:
- cheap furniture catalog
- flashy startup
- SaaS landing page
- wellness brand
- dark luxury showroom
- generic supplier template

---

## 2. Layout density
Use a balanced commercial density:
- enough breathing room to feel premium
- enough efficiency to scan quickly
- not airy-luxury
- not dense-catalog

---

## 3. Container rules
### Desktop
- max outer width: 1200–1280px
- content width: around 1100px
- side padding: 24px

### Mobile
- full width
- side padding: 16px

---

## 4. Section density classes
### Spacious
- Hero
- Why private space matters
- Final CTA

### Medium
- Why pods instead of renovation
- Why Choose Ace
- For offices / designers

### Compact
- Trust strip
- Featured pod types
- Comparison section
- FAQ

Do not apply equal spacing to every section.

---

## 5. Allowed layout patterns
Use only these patterns:
1. Hero split — text left, image right
2. Product grid — 3 product cards
3. Text-led stacked section
4. Image + text split
5. Centered CTA block

Do not invent new layout styles per section.

---

## 6. Section-by-section layout notes
### Hero
Desktop:
- 2-column split
- text 55%
- image 45%

Mobile order:
1. headline
2. subheadline
3. primary CTA
4. secondary CTA
5. hero image
6. proof bullets

### Trust strip
Desktop:
- 4 columns
Mobile:
- 2x2 grid

### Featured pod types
Desktop:
- 3-column product grid
Mobile:
- stacked product cards

### Comparison section
- text-led
- compact stack
- divider-based, not cards

### Why pods instead of renovation
- text-led or mixed
- differentiated from comparison through more breathing room
- can use subtle numeric markers

### Why private space matters
- most editorial of the mid-page sections
- no card styling
- stronger typography and more breathing room

### Why Choose Ace
Desktop:
- 2x2 reassurance grid
Mobile:
- stacked reassurance rows, not stacked cards

### Audience section
Desktop:
- 2-column split
Mobile:
- stacked panels with For offices first and stronger

### FAQ
- single-column accordion
- one open at a time

### Final CTA
- centered block
- light contrast background
- not dark

---

## 7. Typography
Use 1 humanist sans-serif only.

### Desktop type scale
- H1: 48–56px
- H2: 34–40px
- H3: 24–28px
- H4 / card title: 18–22px
- Body large: 18px
- Body: 16px
- Small: 14px
- Micro: 13–14px

### Mobile type scale
- H1: 34–40px
- H2: 28–32px
- H3: 22–24px
- H4: 18–20px
- Body large: 17px
- Body: 15–16px
- Small: 13–14px

### Hard rules
- no all-caps headlines
- sentence case buttons only
- no ultra-light type
- narrow text width in editorial sections
- do not let mobile headings exceed 3–4 lines

---

## 8. Color system
### Roles
- background primary: warm near-white
- background secondary: soft stone
- surface: light warm neutral
- surface alt: very light muted stone / water tint
- text primary: warm charcoal
- text secondary: softened charcoal
- accent: muted water-toned slate
- WhatsApp green only for WhatsApp-specific action

### Hard rules
- no bright blue accents
- no glossy gradients
- no black-black theme
- no brown-heavy earthy interface
- 90% neutral, 10% accent max

---

## 9. Spacing system
Use an 8px base scale:
- 8
- 16
- 24
- 32
- 40
- 48
- 56
- 64
- 80
- 96

Do not make spacing perfectly uniform across all sections.

---

## 10. Buttons
### Primary
- dark charcoal fill
- warm light text
- medium weight
- lightly softened rectangle
- desktop height 48px
- mobile height 50–52px
- radius 12–14px

### Secondary
- transparent or very light surface
- 1px charcoal border
- dark text
- same height and shape as primary

### Hover
- tiny lift
- tiny darken / lighten
- no glow
- no dramatic shadow

### Hard rules
- no pill buttons
- no gradients
- no flashy icon clutter

---

## 11. Component hierarchy
### Strongest visual cards
- Product cards only

### Quiet supporting components
- Reassurance rows
- Audience panels
- FAQ accordion

### Editorial sections
- Comparison
- Renovation
- Private space matters

Do not use the same card style everywhere.

---

## 12. Product cards
Must be the strongest cards on the page.

### Style
- radius: 16–20px
- subtle border
- soft but visible shadow
- image-led
- desktop padding: 24px
- mobile padding: 16–20px

### Hover
- slight lift
- slight image scale
- slightly refined shadow

### Hard rules
- no sale ribbons
- no promo energy
- daily price line must be quiet

---

## 13. Reassurance rows / soft panels
Used for:
- Why Choose Ace
- some mobile audience treatment

### Style
- flatter than product cards
- little to no shadow
- subtle separators or very light panel
- radius: 14–18px

Do not let these look like repeated product cards.

---

## 14. Text-led sections
Used for:
- Comparison section
- Renovation section
- Why private space matters

### Rules
- typography-led
- spacing-led
- divider-led where useful
- avoid boxing everything up

---

## 15. Images
Use a mix of:
- product-only shots
- office-context shots
- in-use shots with a person

### Editing style
- warm-neutral
- soft natural light
- no blue cast
- no orange-heavy cast
- no fake glossy CGI look

### Cropping
- hero: environmental and premium
- product cards: cleaner and tighter
- lower sections: selective office context

---

## 16. Motion
Allowed:
- fade
- slight rise
- slight hover lift
- smooth accordion
- subtle image scale on product cards

Not allowed:
- parallax
- glow
- dramatic motion
- animation chains

Motion should be calm and controlled.

---

## 17. Mobile rules
### Mandatory
- full-width stacked buttons
- hero order stays as defined above
- no sticky CTA in v1
- not a simple stacked desktop clone

### Section-specific
- product cards remain cards
- Why Choose Ace becomes reassurance rows
- comparison section stays compact and divider-based
- private space stays spacious and editorial
- offices block stays visually primary over designers

---

## 18. Borrow / do not borrow from premium reference sites
### Borrow
- image-led premium feel
- restrained typography
- narrow text widths
- alternating image/text rhythm
- mix of isolated product + real context imagery
- consistent desktop/mobile visual language

### Do not borrow
- overly soft conversion structure
- tiny delicate support text for key commercial info
- lifestyle storytelling at the expense of CTA clarity
- equal visual softness across all sections
