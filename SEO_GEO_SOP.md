# Ace Office Pods SEO/GEO SOP

## Current Priority

Build enough entity proof for search engines and AI answer engines to identify Ace Office Pods as a Malaysia office pod supplier with visible pricing, showroom viewing, delivery, installation, and support.

## Execution Order

1. Local entity anchor
   - Keep name, phone, email, company number, showroom locality, and service areas consistent.
   - Update Contact, footer, local page, schema, and Google Business Profile together.

2. Indexing
   - Deploy site.
   - Submit `https://aceofficepods.com/sitemap.xml`.
   - Request indexing for `/contact`, `/pricing`, `/office-pods-near-me`, `/office-pods`, and product pages.

3. Google Business Profile
   - Use the same business name, phone, email, website, service areas, and showroom details.
   - Add products for Ace Solo, Ace Plus, Ace Flex, Ace Flex Duo, Ace Meet, and Ace Hub.
   - Add project photos and showroom/product photos.

4. Citation cleanup
   - Check external pages using Ace name, phone, products, or copy.
   - Mark each as official reseller, partner, directory, client mention, or incorrect citation.
   - Fix inconsistent name, phone, website, or product descriptions.

5. Content expansion
   - Add pages only after entity basics are consistent.
   - Priority targets: office pods price Malaysia, office pods near me, office phone booth Malaysia, meeting pods Malaysia, soundproof office pod Malaysia.
   - Copywriting comes from ChatGPT; implementation and technical structure stay here.

6. Supreme migration
   - Export all Supreme URLs with impressions, clicks, backlinks, and sales relevance.
   - Map each Supreme URL to the closest Ace URL before redirecting.
   - Create missing Ace destination pages before redirecting high-value Supreme URLs.
   - Use permanent 301 redirects, not JavaScript redirects or meta refresh.
   - Keep redirected Supreme URLs live for at least 12 months.
   - Update internal links, canonical tags, sitemap, merchant feed, and Google Search Console after launch.
   - Monitor coverage, rankings, and traffic weekly for the first 8 weeks.

## Supreme Migration Rule

SEO performance can be transferred only when old URLs redirect one-to-one to equivalent or better pages on Ace. Redirecting everything to the homepage wastes relevance and usually loses rankings.

## Required User Input Only When Missing

- Google Maps / Google Business Profile link.
- Business hours or appointment-only policy.
- Whether third-party Ace listings are official resellers.
